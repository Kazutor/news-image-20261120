Kyiv · Overnight

Missiles strike Kyiv’s bridges over the Dnipro

Russia fired ballistic missiles at the Ukrainian capital overnight, damaging three bridges across the Dnipro river and cutting power and water in parts of the city. Traffic on the crossings was blocked as engineers inspected the damage.

A drone also struck a hospital in Kharkiv, injuring four people.

A bridge span over the Dnipro is hit in the overnight barrage; the Northern, Podilsky and Southern bridges were damaged.
Odisha, India · This morning

Cyclone Akvan comes ashore in Odisha

Severe Cyclone Akvan made landfall near Gopalpur on Friday morning, with winds of 70–90 km/h. About 300,000 people were moved to shelters.

Riyadh · Thursday evening

Missile hits Riyadh’s tallest tower

A suspected Houthi missile damaged the PIF Tower, home of Saudi Arabia’s wealth fund, blowing out windows across seven to eight floors. No injuries were reported.

Wall Street · Thursday

Anthropic jumps 17% in record IPO debut

Shares in the AI lab Anthropic closed at $97.17 on their first day on Nasdaq, after the biggest IPO ever raised $81bn. US stocks still fell for a fifth straight day.

Tokyo · Figures out today

Japan’s core inflation holds at 2.2%

Japan’s core consumer prices rose 2.2% in October from a year earlier, data released on Friday showed — still above the Bank of Japan’s 2% target.

Senegal · Search under way

Four dead as migrant boat capsizes off Senegal

Four bodies were recovered after a boat carrying 50 to 100 people capsized near Yenne, south of Dakar; dozens are still missing. Arrivals in Spain’s Canary Islands are up nearly two-thirds on last year.
